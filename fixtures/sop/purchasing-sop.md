# Standard Operating Procedure — Purchasing

**Document:** SOP-FIN-014
**Version:** 3.1
**Owner:** Finance & Operations
**Effective date:** 1 March 2026

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure governs every purchase of goods and services made on behalf of
the company by an employee. It applies to all departments and to all amounts.
Purchases outside this procedure are not reimbursed.

## 2. Definitions

**Request.** A written statement of what is needed and why, submitted through
the purchasing system.

**Request amount.** The total value of a request in euros, excluding tax,
calculated from the approved catalog price of each item multiplied by the
requested quantity.

**Approver.** A member of the Finance & Operations team authorised to approve or
reject a request.

## 3. Eligible items

3.1 Only items listed in the approved company catalog may be purchased through
this procedure.

3.2 A request that names an item which is not in the catalog must not proceed.
The requester is asked to submit a new request naming a catalog item.

3.3 Quantities must be whole units and greater than zero.

## 4. Spending thresholds

4.1 A request whose amount is **500 EUR or less** does not require approval. It
proceeds directly to order.

4.2 A request whose amount is **more than 500 EUR** requires the approval of an
Approver before an order is created.

4.3 Thresholds apply to the request as a whole, not to individual line items. A
request must not be split into smaller requests in order to stay below a
threshold.

## 5. Prohibited categories

5.1 Gift cards, prepaid cards and cash equivalents must not be purchased through
this procedure under any circumstances, regardless of amount. Such a request is
rejected outright and is not eligible for approval.

5.2 A request that contains a prohibited item is rejected in full. The remaining
items are not purchased; the requester submits a new request without the
prohibited item.

## 6. Incomplete or ambiguous requests

6.1 A request that does not state what is to be purchased, or states it in terms
that cannot be matched to a catalog item, must not proceed.

6.2 Where this procedure does not clearly cover a request, the request must not
proceed. Authorisation is never assumed. The requester is told what is missing
and submits a new request.

## 7. Record keeping

7.1 Every request records the version of this procedure that was in force when
it was submitted.

7.2 A revision of this procedure does not change a request that was already
submitted, including one awaiting approval.
